Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

auto clipper ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the ClipsGG204 YouTube channel. It is used only to upload video clips to that channel.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to your YouTube account using the YouTube Data API. It does not read your email, contacts, or any other Google data.

How information is used and stored

The sign-in token Google provides is stored only on the computer where the app runs, and is used only to upload videos you choose. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on your computer also removes the stored access.
