Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

auto clipper ("the app") is a desktop tool operated by the owner of the ClipsGG204 channel. It is used to upload short video clips to connected YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: with your permission, basic profile info (user.info.basic) to identify the connected account, and permission to publish videos (video.publish) through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) through the YouTube Data API.

The app does not read your messages, contacts, followers, email or any other account data.

How information is used and stored

Sign-in tokens from TikTok and Google are stored only on the computer where the app runs and are used only to upload the clips you choose. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token files on your computer also removes the stored access.
