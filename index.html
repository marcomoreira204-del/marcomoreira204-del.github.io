auto clipper is a desktop tool used by the ClipsGG204 gaming channel to publish short gaming clips. It cuts highlight clips from gameplay videos and posts them, with a title and caption, to the channel's own accounts on YouTube (youtube.com/@ClipsGG204) and TikTok.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and Google to give the tool permission to post.
  2. The tool cuts a gameplay video into short vertical clips and writes a title and caption for each.
  3. Once a day it uploads the next clip to the connected accounts using the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API, using the privacy setting the account allows.

The tool only posts to accounts that have signed in and given permission. It does not read messages, followers or any other account data.
